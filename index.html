About

About Me.

Curious by nature, rigorous by practice — with a bias toward making complex things feel simple.

I’m Aliya S, a second-year BCA student specializing in AI and Data Science at Ilahia College of Arts and Science (2025-2029). I enjoy turning raw data into clear insights and building models that help people make better decisions.

I work mainly in Python and SQL, using Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, and Scikit-learn for data cleaning, exploratory analysis, visualization, and machine learning. My projects include a student dropout prediction model built on more than 79,000 records, a sales data analysis, and a student performance study. I also built and deployed PricePrism, a full-stack web app that compares product prices across online stores and highlights the cheapest option.

I recently completed a one-month Data Science Internship at Codemax Digital Solutions, where I applied these skills to real datasets. I hold certifications from Google (Data Analytics and AI) and Simplilearn (Python for Data Science, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning).

Beyond academics, I’m IPR Lead at my college’s IEDC, a state-level volleyball participant, and a competitor in the Hackspire 24-hour ideathon.

I’m open to internships and entry-level roles in data analysis, AI/ML, and software engineering.

02 / PREDICTIVE

Student Dropout Prediction

Analyzed GPA, attendance, financial stress, work hours, and LMS activity across 79,000+ student records to predict dropout risk. Compared two models on an imbalanced dataset: Random Forest reached 90.8% accuracy, while Logistic Regression caught 64.7% of actual dropouts (recall) versus 24.2%. Top predictors: semester GPA, financial stress, work hours, attendance.

pandasnumpyscikit-learn
